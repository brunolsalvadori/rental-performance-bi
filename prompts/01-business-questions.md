# 01 — Business questions

> Reconstructed from my project notes. The prompt is summarized, not verbatim.

## Goal
Decide who the BI is for and which questions it must answer before building anything.

## Prompt
*Summary:* Build a management dashboard for the cabin from our management spreadsheet and the Airbnb reports. The partners need to see whether the operation is healthy and how guests rate it, in one place, on their phones.

## What came back
A first dashboard covering revenue, costs, occupancy and platform metrics.

## What I kept, changed or rejected
- **Kept — two audiences, two kinds of question.** Partners ask about money: profit after costs, the acquisition loan, and who owes whom. The manager asks about operations: occupancy, channels, and what guests praise or complain about. This became the two views of the product (see the question table in the main README).
- **Kept — financing and the balance between partners as a section of their own.** Settlements between partners are the most sensitive number in the operation, so they are not buried in a cost table.
- **Kept — guest reviews with suggested actions.** Beyond the rating, the dashboard groups praise and points of attention and proposes an action for each recurring complaint, so the manager knows what to fix.
- **Rejected — PDF output.** The dashboard is shared as a link or file that opens on a phone, not as a document to print.
